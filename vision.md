# Vision — the ab0t bot store

## Why this repo exists

Most teams that come to the mesh are not trying to learn the mesh. They have a product, a deadline and a
handful of jobs they would rather not become experts in: letting people sign in, deciding who can do what,
charging for what gets used, sending the email, running the sandbox, calling the model. The mesh already
does those jobs. What a customer is missing is someone who knows it well, sitting next to them, getting
them from "we want this" to "it works in our app" — and staying around for what comes after.

This repo is that someone, many times over. Each bot is a specialist colleague for one part of the mesh:
it knows the service, speaks the customer's language, does the setup with them, and is still useful a
month later when something needs changing. Together they are a **bot store**: the place a business goes
to get help with any service on the mesh — ours, or a third party's that lives on the mesh with us.

## What our customers want from it

From the customer's chair, the wish list is short and very human:

- **Outcomes, not plumbing.** "My users can sign in." "Free and Pro tiers, with an upgrade button."
  "Agents in my product can send email." They want to name the result and get there.
- **Fast first success.** Something real working in their own app on day one — a login page with their
  logo, a key that works, a test customer that can do the right things.
- **One way in to everything.** The same feel whether they are wiring identity, billing, compute or a
  partner's API. Learn one bot, and the next one is familiar.
- **Help that stays.** Day two matters as much as day zero: add a plan, invite a teammate, rotate a key,
  connect a new service, understand why a customer is stuck.
- **Their pace, their call.** Guidance when they want it, a straight answer when they don't, and nothing
  happening behind their back.
- **To own what they built.** The configuration lives in their repo, the steps are pages they can read
  without the bot, and nothing traps them in a tool.
- **Their customers to have a good time.** The real goal is the people who use *their* product — fast
  sign-up, fair limits, a clear upgrade, a quick fix when something goes wrong.

## What the bot store is, over time

1. **A shelf of specialists.** One bot per job a customer has: getting on the mesh (Ally), money and
   usage (Penny), access inside their app (Zee), and more as the mesh grows — audit, integrations,
   compute, tools, events, models, communication, go-live, support.
2. **Open to the whole mesh.** Third-party services on the mesh can publish a bot for their own product,
   built the same way (Meta shows how). A customer finds help for a partner's service in the same place,
   in the same style, as help for ours.
3. **Bots that work together.** A customer starts anywhere; each bot knows the others and passes the
   conversation to the right one, so the customer never has to know where one service ends and the next
   begins.
4. **Grown from real use.** Every question a bot cannot yet answer, and every step a service cannot yet
   do, is written down. That list is how the mesh and its bots get better, in the order customers need.
5. **For businesses whose users are agents.** More and more of the people calling these services will
   be agents acting for someone. The store grows with that: bots that help customers give their agents
   an identity, a budget, rules and a human to ask.

## The values we align on

- **Start from the customer's goal.** Every bot begins with what the customer is trying to achieve and
  works back to the mesh, never the other way round.
- **Plain language.** Talk about customers, plans, invoices and teams. Bring in the technical word only
  when it helps the customer do something.
- **The customer stays in charge.** Bots explain, show what they will do, and act when asked.
- **Teach while doing.** A customer should finish a session understanding their own setup a little
  better, and be able to do the next step alone if they want.
- **One family.** Every bot looks, sounds and behaves alike, so trust earned with one carries to the next.
- **Small, useful steps.** Each page does one job well. Short beats complete; useful today beats perfect
  next quarter.
- **Open by default.** The procedures are readable by anyone, partners can contribute bots, and a
  customer can always see how things work.
- **Built to be kept.** Bots are maintained like products: they keep pace with the services they serve,
  and they say so when something is not there yet.

## What success looks like

- A new customer goes from "never heard of the mesh" to a working sign-in, a first plan and a first
  paying customer in an afternoon.
- Customers come back to the bots for day-two work, not only for setup.
- Partners on the mesh ship a bot alongside their service as a matter of course.
- Questions customers used to bring to our team are answered by a bot, and the questions bots cannot
  answer become the next thing we build.
